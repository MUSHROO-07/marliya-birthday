A LETTER FOR YOU 26 / 10 / 2026

Assalamu Alaikum, Marliya.

A few words from
my heart to yours.

Happy birthday, Marliya.

It is hard to put a lifetime of love into a few lines, but today I want you to know how grateful I am that Allah chose you to be my sister. No matter how much we grow or how quickly life changes, you will always have a special place in my heart.

May Allah bless your life with good health, peace in your heart, strength in difficult moments, and happiness that brings you closer to Him. May He protect you from what harms you, guide every step you take, accept your duas, and place barakah in your time, your dreams, and your future.

I pray that you are surrounded by sincere people, that your heart always finds its way back to peace, and that the things meant for you reach you at the right time. May Allah grant you goodness in this life and the Hereafter, and keep our family together in love and mercy.

You do not need to be perfect or have everything figured out. Keep being yourself, keep your faith close, and remember that you are loved more than these words can explain.

With love and duas, always,
Your brother, Mubeen
